Editorial Standards

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This page documents the sourcing, verification, independence, and review standards applied to every article, newsletter, and piece of analysis published on Succession Holding LLC. The standards exist to make our work legible to readers who want to know what kind of publication they are reading, how we verify what we publish, and where the boundaries are between editorial content and anything else.

What This Site Is

Succession Holding LLC is an independent real estate education publication. The content is produced by a small editorial team whose work draws on direct operating experience with residential and recreational rental property in Colorado, New Hampshire, Alaska, New Mexico, and New York. Every property referenced on this site is owned through a single-purpose entity wholly owned by the holding company. The publication exists to share what the editorial team has learned from that operating experience with independent landlords and prospective investors who are making decisions about small-scale rental real estate.

Sourcing Standards

Numerical claims, statistics, regulatory facts, and market observations are tied to named primary sources whenever the underlying data is available from a primary source. The publication draws regularly on the following categories of primary sources:

  • Federal statistical agencies. U.S. Census Bureau for housing inventory, vacancy, rental market, and demographic data. Bureau of Labor Statistics for inflation, employment, and wages. Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) for interest rates, mortgage rates, and yield-curve data.
  • Federal regulators. Federal Trade Commission, Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, Department of Housing and Urban Development, Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, Federal Housing Finance Agency, and the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network for regulatory actions, rule changes, and enforcement context.
  • Federal and state law enforcement. Federal Bureau of Investigation Internet Crime Complaint Center, state Attorneys General, and state Departments of Financial Institutions for fraud alerts, consumer advisories, and enforcement actions.
  • State and county recorders. Secretary of State offices for entity standing and corporate filings. County recorder and assessor offices for transaction data, deed records, and property tax assessments.
  • Rent and market data providers. Apartment List, Apartments.com, Redfin, Zillow Research, and CoreLogic for rent levels, vacancy, and price trends. Where provider methodology differs, the article names the provider.
  • Trade press and primary-source publications. Federal Register for final rules, Mortgage Bankers Association for origination and delinquency data, National Association of Realtors for existing-home sales, and U.S. Census New Residential Construction for housing starts and permits.

Two-Source Verification

Every quantitative claim, factual assertion about a regulatory action, and named-event reference is confirmed against at least two independent sources before publication. Where only one source exists (a state-specific statutory provision, a county-level assessment practice, a single-source dataset), the article names the source explicitly and the limitation. Where two sources disagree, the article names both and notes the discrepancy. Where verification fails, the claim is dropped.

This standard applies to the daily newsletter as rigorously as it does to long-form analysis. Specific FBI or DOJ case numbers, cap rate figures, median rent figures, agency press release dates, and market-specific inventory counts are all subject to the same verification standard. A claim that cannot be verified is not published.

Independence Policy

Succession Holding LLC is independently financed by its owner. Editorial decisions are made by the editorial team. No advertiser, sponsor, lender, broker, vendor, affiliate partner, or other commercial entity has any role in selecting topics, framing analysis, choosing sources, or approving content before publication. The only financial relationships on this site are:

  • Standard Google AdSense display advertising, clearly labeled "Advertisement" above every ad unit per AdSense policy.
  • Affiliate links where relevant and disclosed inline at the point of the link.

The publication does not accept sponsored content, paid placements, native advertising, or any form of compensation contingent on editorial coverage. The publication does not promote specific properties, brokerages, lenders, vendors, or investment products in exchange for compensation. When a property or service is referenced editorially, the reference is selected by the editorial team on the basis of relevance to readers.

Editorial Review Process

Every article and newsletter issue passes through a defined review before publication. The process consists of:

  1. Drafting against a written structure (intro, the day's lens, source citations, action item, conclusion).
  2. Source verification against the two-source standard above. Inline citations are checked against the original publication.
  3. Editorial review for structural coherence, claim traceability, and plain-language accessibility. Technical terms are defined on first use.
  4. AdSense and disclosure compliance check to ensure every ad slot is correctly emitted, the visible "Advertisement" label is above every ad unit, and the disclosure language on disclaimer and terms pages matches the actual publishing practices.
  5. Live verification after deploy: the published URL is fetched, the meta description is checked, the canonical URL is checked, and the rendered word count is confirmed against the issue's type-conditional floor.

Voice and Sourcing Boundaries

Articles and newsletters are written in a single voice under the byline "Succession Editorial." No individual staff bylines appear on the publication. Every issue reads as the work of one editorial team, not as the opinions of individual contributors. Articles are addressed to independent landlords and prospective investors who underwrite their own deals at small scale. The publication is not written for institutional asset managers, flippers, or syndicators and does not pitch services to those audiences.

The publication distinguishes clearly between editorial content and any reference to external services, vendors, or products. When a product or service is named, the reference is editorial and not compensated. Affiliate links, where used, are marked and never appear in a Sunday "one action" newsletter.

Corrections Policy

Errors are corrected when found. When a correction is material (a numerical claim, a regulatory reference, a named source, a date), the correction is made inline at the point of the original claim, with a dated note describing what was changed and why. When a correction is minor (a typo, a punctuation issue, a link fix), the correction is made silently. The publication date and last-modified date are visible in the article header.

Primary-Source Categories Used in the Daily Brief

The Succession Daily Brief, published every weekday morning, draws on the following primary-source categories on a rotating basis. The rotation is documented in the operating procedure to ensure no single primary source dominates the publication's coverage.

  • Federal statistical agencies (Census, BLS, FRED) for market and macro data.
  • Federal regulators (FTC, CFPB, HUD, FinCEN) for rule changes, enforcement actions, and consumer advisories.
  • Federal and state law enforcement (FBI IC3, state Attorneys General, state DFIs) for fraud alerts and consumer warnings.
  • State and county recorders for entity, deed, and property data.
  • Rent and market data providers for current rent levels, vacancy, and trend lines.
  • Federal Register, trade press, and primary-source publications for final rules, origination data, and sector research.

What This Site Is Not

The publication is for educational and informational purposes. Readers making specific investment, financing, tax, or legal decisions should consult licensed professionals who have reviewed their individual situation.

The publication does not provide personalized investment guidance, property recommendations, deal underwriting, or entity structuring advice. The publication is not a registered investment adviser, real estate brokerage, or legal practice.
